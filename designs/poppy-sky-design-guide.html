Responsive layout

The Solution section uses four open, illustrated columns on desktop and adapts to the screen width.

Desktop

Above 900 px, four tools sit side by side. The journey illustration stays beside the scrollable steps.

Tablet

At 900 px and below, the tools shift to two columns and the journey illustration sits above the steps.

Phone

At 600 px and below, each tool gets one row with its illustration alongside the copy.
