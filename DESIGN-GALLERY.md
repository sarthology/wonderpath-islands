# WonderPath design gallery

The four-direction design gallery is at `design-gallery.html`. The root page is the approved island explorer. All pages are static and use relative links so GitHub Pages works under /wonderpath-islands/.

- designs/bold.html — Electric Play: cobalt, acid lime, lilac and coral.
- designs/open-world.html — Open World: sky blue, coral, navy and an island panorama.
- designs/dreamscape.html — Dreamscape: faceted sunset scenery, clouds and a twilight journey.
- designs/graphics-refresh.html — Original, reimagined: current wonderpath.ai layout with two new faceted illustrations.

Every direction has a fixed All designs link. The vertical comparison at `vertical.html` links to the horizontal explorer at the root. Visits to `islands.html` redirect to the explorer.

The rejected warm-ivory concept and rejected logo are excluded. Existing landing-page copy is retained across the four directions. Forms display preview feedback and direct visitors to wonderpath.ai rather than collecting email.

Embedded images and fonts were extracted into shared static assets. Existing island assets are reused when their bytes match. Preview images are genuine captures of each direction.

The two new graphics were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Their prompts are retained in GRAPHICS-REFRESH-PROMPTS.md. WebP originals are included among the shared assets.
